## Proposal

<Scale values={[24, 28, 32, 36]} />

<Story title="Height tokens" kind="proposed" note="Menu items use 32px in every popover (48px with a description) and 36px in the command palette. List rows use 36px for one line and 56px for two.">

<Finding>
  <Rule>Every button, input, chip, tab, and single-line menu item fits one of four heights. The fixed exceptions are the checkbox (16), the shortcut key (20), and the command palette’s search field (40). If two controls share a bar (header, row, toolbar), they share a height. The sidebar goes from 31 to 32; tabs and Filter, from 31 to 28; Settings buttons, from 30 to 32; the trial button, from 37 to 32.</Rule>
</Finding>
