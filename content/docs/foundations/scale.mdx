## What works well

Worth saying before the outliers:

- Interface text almost always stays between 12 and 15px.
- Right-panel and Settings cards share a 12 radius and a 0.5px #313131 border.
- The primary button is the same across the app: 32px, radius 7, blue #4262FF.
- The sidebar is very regular: 31px items with radius 7 and 2px between them.

The proposal starts from those values. Almost everything proposed already exists somewhere in Circleback; the work is choosing one version and applying it everywhere.
