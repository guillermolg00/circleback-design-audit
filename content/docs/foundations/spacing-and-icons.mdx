## Spacing

### Current

<Scale values={[1.5, 2, 3.5, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24]} />

- The **1.5 and 3.5px** gaps live in the sidebar (3.5 between the workspace icon and text); the items themselves are 2px apart.
- A **10px** gap separates the checkbox from the text in action items; in every other row, the avatar sits 8px away.
- Settings row paddings are **11px 19px**, off the 4px grid. In Tags they become 16px 19px.
- Spacing between blocks varies too: from title to content there are 12, 16, 17, 29, and 27px depending on the page (see [Page header](/docs/components/page-header)).
