title: Roadmap
description: Fifteen changes in three stages, from cheapest to most structural.
---

<Steps>
<Step>
### Quick fixes · 1–2 weeks

1. Errors with a cause and an action; Delete moves to the menu; titles with dates. → [Dead-end errors](/docs/ux/errors)
2. One set of actions per meeting; “Delete” everywhere. → [Menus per object](/docs/ux/menus)
3. Visible filter chips, including those in “Mine”. → [Invisible filters](/docs/ux/filters)
4. Shortcuts: `?` opens help, without overriding ⌘X or ⌘G; J/K in every list. → [Half-working keyboard](/docs/ux/keyboard)
5. Empty states with one CTA and consistent copy. → [Empty state](/docs/components/empty-state)
6. Only one upsell surface at a time. → [Too much upsell](/docs/ux/upsell)
</Step>
<Step>
### System · 2–4 weeks

7. Radius, height, border, gray, and icon tokens in the theme. → [Tokens](/docs/proposal/tokens)
8. Migrate ListRow, Menu, Chip, Button, Card, Kbd, Search, and Checkbox behind a visual diff. → [Component rules](/docs/proposal/rules)
9. A single section template in Settings. → [Settings](/docs/components/settings)
10. Document: 15/24 in Notes and Transcript. → [Notes and transcript](/docs/components/notes-and-transcript)
11. Accessible labels on every icon button. → [Naming and accessibility](/docs/ux/naming-and-accessibility)
</Step>
<Step>
### Experience · 1–2 months

12. Contextual ⌘K with visible shortcuts. → [Context-blind command palette](/docs/ux/contextual-palette)
13. Home as “what’s on my plate today.” → [Home and action items](/docs/ux/home-and-action-items)
14. Action items with dates, grouping, and a link to the transcript.
15. Adaptive layout: collapsible panel and a visible player at medium widths; shared meetings with the same structure and explained permissions. → [Layout at medium widths](/docs/ux/layout) · [Shared meetings](/docs/ux/shared-meetings)
</Step>
</Steps>
