## What was measured

Everything was measured on the real app, in production, with `getComputedStyle` and `getBoundingClientRect`: sizes, radii, borders, gaps, paddings, and the visible distance between blocks. The audit covered:

- **10 screens:** Home, Meetings, Action Items, Automations, Insights, Ask, People, Companies, meeting detail (Notes and Transcript), and a shared meeting.
- **6 Settings sections:** Account, Preferences, Calendar, Sharing, Emails, and Tags.
- **8 overlays:** the “…” menu, the right-click menu, the account menu, New meeting, Filter, ⌘K, the shortcuts panel, and the Share dialog. The tooltip and the selection bar were measured as controls (see the [Appendix](/docs/appendix)).

## How it was captured

Screenshots were taken at **800px wide** so they render at 1:1 scale, except those that needed the right panel (1068, 1180, and 1408px). The pink annotations are redlines drawn on the live page, with measured values, not estimates.

Personal data (meeting titles, third-party names, emails, note content, and photos) is blurred. This was a read-only review: nothing was created, modified, or deleted.

## Limits

- Only few meetings and a single workspace.
- Dark mode only. Light mode and the desktop and mobile apps were not reviewed.
- Measurements come from a specific version of the app (September 29, 2026); some may have changed since.
