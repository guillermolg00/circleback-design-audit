---
title: Invisible filters
description: Tabs apply filters you can’t see, and there are no display options.
---

<Sev level="medium" />

<Finding>
  <What>
    The “Mine” tab in Action Items applies Assignee and Status under the hood. The Filter menu shows a “1” on each, but the list has no chips to indicate it, and completed items disappear without a hint. The default tab is “All” in Meetings and “Mine” in Action Items. There are no display options either (group, sort, show completed).
  </What>
  <Linear>
    Active filters appear as chips in the bar, removable one by one, and Display options controls grouping, sorting, and which properties to show.
  </Linear>
  <Proposal>
    Visible, removable filter chips, including the ones a tab applies, and a Display menu with group, sort, and show completed. The same default tab in every list.
  </Proposal>
</Finding>

<Figure eager src="/img/u07-hidden-filters.jpg" alt="Filter menu with Assignee and Status active and no sign of them in the list" width={572} height={345} caption="Two active filters that don’t show in the list." />
