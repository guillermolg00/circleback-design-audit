## What already works well

Before the findings, here is what is worth keeping:

- ⌘K with Tab to hand off to the AI, plus navigation to any Settings section.
- Shortcuts panel (⌘/) with a search field.
- Selection bar with actions for multi-select, and right-click on rows.
- Filters with a search field, and a well-organized Settings.
- People as a list with the detail alongside, navigable with J/K.
- The “New meeting” menu explains each option.
- Tooltips show the shortcut (Filter → F).
