---
title: Half-working keyboard
description: Good shortcuts, unevenly applied and hard to discover.
---

<Sev level="medium" />

<Finding>
  <What>
    The shortcuts panel (⌘/) promises J/K in lists, but in Meetings J/K didn’t move the selection; in People it did. `?` focuses the assistant, while the convention (Linear, GitHub, Gmail) is to open help. `⌘X` creates a clip and overrides Cut; `⌘G` copies a link and overrides Find Next. F opens filters or goes full screen depending on context. Tooltips do show shortcuts (Filter → F), but menus and ⌘K don’t.
  </What>
  <Linear>
    Single-letter shortcuts for actions on the object (S status, A assign, L labels), G plus a letter to navigate, and the shortcut visible in tooltips, menus, and ⌘K. None of them overrides a system shortcut.
  </Linear>
  <Proposal>
    Make `?` open help. Move Create clip and Copy link to free combinations (⇧⌘X and ⇧⌘C). Make J/K work in every list. Show the shortcut on every menu item and in ⌘K, and add G + letter navigation (G M meetings, G A action items).
  </Proposal>
</Finding>
