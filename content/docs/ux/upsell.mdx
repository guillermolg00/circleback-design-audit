---
title: Too much upsell
description: Four sales surfaces at once on the same screen.
---

<Sev level="medium" />

<Finding>
  <What>
    Home packs in a trial banner (1), a “6 days left on Pro” button (2), a “Better together” card (3), and “See email conversations” (4), which also repeats in the meeting and in People. Ask adds “connect your apps”, and ⌘K adds “Copy referral link”.
  </What>
  <Why>
    Every surface competes with the content for attention. The same trial message appears twice on screen.
  </Why>
  <Linear>
    A single dismissible card (“What’s new”) at the bottom of the sidebar.
  </Linear>
  <Proposal>
    One upsell surface at a time, in priority order (trial, team, integrations), that doesn’t come back once dismissed. Trial status lives in one place: the bottom of the sidebar.
  </Proposal>
</Finding>
