---
title: Layout at medium widths
description: The right panel doesn’t collapse and the player disappears.
---

<Sev level="medium" />

<Finding>
  <What>
    At around 1070px, the right panel (288px) stays open and the notes column shrinks to 349px: action items get cut off after a few words. The video player appears next to the transcript at 1408px, but at around 1070px it drops out of view.
  </What>
  <Linear>
    The details panel hides with a toggle and collapses on its own below a certain width. The main content always keeps a readable minimum.
  </Linear>
  <Proposal>
    Collapse the right panel below about 1200px (the `]` shortcut already exists) and move the player into a fixed bar above the transcript at medium widths. Set a 560px minimum for the document column.
  </Proposal>
</Finding>
