## Current

Row hover has the same height (58px, up to 60 in Meetings) and radius (12px) in Meetings, People, Companies, and Home, but **how far it bleeds** past the content column changes: 34px on the left and 32 on the right in Meetings, 8px in People and Companies, and 8 and 40px in Home. Switching between lists, the column “jumps.”
