## Current

Visually, Meetings and People are almost aligned (16 and 17px), though they are built differently underneath: one uses tabs, the other a 48px block that centers the Filter. The outliers are **Home (29px)**, **Settings (27px)**, and the **meeting detail (12px)**. On top of that, the header is 30px without a button and 32px with a primary button, so the Filter sits 2px lower in Action Items than in Meetings.
