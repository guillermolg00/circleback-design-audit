## Current

The primary button is the same across the app: **32px, radius 7, blue**, in Share, New action item, and New automation. Secondary buttons follow no rule.

<Finding>
  <Rule>A button is md in headers, forms, and dialogs, and sm inside rows, cards, and filter bars. New meeting moves to md as a secondary button with a strong border, and the trial button to an md ghost button; Settings goes from 30 to 32; Scroll to now and Send drop the pill shape.</Rule>
